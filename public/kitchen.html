Shopping for

How the math works: pick the days you're shopping for above — every meal on those days is added together, so one trip covers them all. Each item is bought for the groups it's assigned to, at its “per person” amount (a planning average — skippers and seconds baked in); meat is raw purchase weight; each day's buffer goes on top. With Subtract pantry on, stock comes off the combined total once. Check items off as you shop, then close the trip out at the foot of the list.

Day

The pantry is a standing ledger: it belongs to the kitchen, not to any one day. Enter what's on the shelves (lb for weight, pc for counted items) and add anything Daniel has, whether or not it's on a menu. Stock is matched to menu items by name and subtracted once from whatever days you're shopping for. “Needs” below reflects the days selected on the Shopping list tab.

Everyone on this page shares one board — edits save automatically and reach the others within a few seconds. Portions are starting points from standard catering planning ranges; trust Daniel's read of his crowd over the defaults.
