Shopping for

How the math works: pick the days you're shopping for above — quantities for every day are added together, so one trip covers them all. Each “per person” number is a planning average across the group it applies to (skippers and seconds baked in); meat is raw purchase weight; each day's buffer is added on top. With Subtract pantry on, stock is taken off the combined total once — fully covered items read “stocked ✓”. Check items off as you shop; checked and stocked items drop off the printout. Edit portions and menus on the Menus tab.

Day

The pantry is a standing ledger: it belongs to the kitchen, not to any one day. Enter what's on the shelves (lb for weight, pc for counted items) and add anything Daniel has, whether or not it's on a menu. Stock is matched to menu items by name and subtracted once from whatever days you're shopping for. “List needs” below reflects the days selected on the Shopping list tab.
